Fog of War

A free League of Legends companion app for Windows · Türkçe ve English

Download for Windows
  • Champion select: your teammates' ranks and form, ban and pick suggestions, lane matchups.
  • Scouting window (Alt+X) while the game loads, and a small in-game overlay.
  • Profiles: your match history with full scoreboards, LP graph, and any player's profile by Riot ID.

FOW only reads from the League client and never changes anything in it. It shows nothing the game hides: players kept anonymous in champion select stay hidden. No accounts, no telemetry.
